\def\themes{
    I study the principles of neural computation, developing new analytical methods to connect noisy, high-dimensional recordings to theory from statistical physics and dynamical systems:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin = 1.5em, topsep = 3pt, itemsep = 1pt, parsep = 0pt]
        \item \textbf{Spatiotemporal dynamics}: how nested traveling waves coordinate hierarchical neural processing and flexible behavior.
        \item \textbf{Criticality}: robust methods to track the distance to criticality in noisy, poorly sampled systems.

        \item \textbf{Population geometry}: low-dimensional coding, communication subspaces, and dimensionality reduction.
    \end{itemize}
