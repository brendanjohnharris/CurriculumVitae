\section{Research themes}
\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{\textwidth}\small
I study how collective dynamics in neural populations perform computation, combining large-scale electrophysiology with statistical physics and dynamical systems theory. My methods rely on uncovering structure from noisy, high-dimensional, heavily subsampled recordings.\\[3pt]
\hspace*{1.5em}\textbullet\ \ \textbf{Criticality and computation}: tracking distance to a critical point when the noise process is unknown and sampling is sparse.\\[1pt]
\hspace*{1.5em}\textbullet\ \ \textbf{Spatiotemporal dynamics}: nested traveling waves that coordinate hierarchical processing and communication between brain areas.\\[1pt]
\hspace*{1.5em}\textbullet\ \ \textbf{Stochastic processes}: fractional diffusion for spiking-circuit mean field theory, decision making, and adaptive visual sampling.\\[1pt]
\hspace*{1.5em}\textbullet\ \ \textbf{Population geometry}: coding and communication subspaces, dimensionality reduction for large-scale electrophysiology data.
\end{minipage}\par\vspace{2pt}

\section{Technical skills}
\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{\textwidth}\small
\textbf{Neural data}: high-density extracellular electrophysiology (Neuropixels, multi-electrode arrays); spike trains, LFP, and EEG; spectral, phase, and traveling-wave analysis; criticality and phase-transition analysis.\\[4pt]
\textbf{Statistics and modeling}: dimensionality reduction; stochastic processes and statistical physics; spiking neural networks; mean-field reductions; feature-based time-series analysis (\emph{hctsa}, \emph{catch22}) and machine-learning.\\[4pt]
\textbf{Engineering}: Julia, Python, MATLAB, C++; real-time and closed-loop systems; hardware interfacing; HPC and large-scale data pipelines; reproducible analysis, package release, and long-term maintenance.
\end{minipage}\par\vspace{2pt}
